# NUYE

**Make knowledge easier to understand.** NUYE is an article platform for people who want to learn about technology and people who want to teach it. Combine writing with diagrams, code, and speech bubbles to explain ideas in your own way.

[日本語版](./README.md)

## What you can do with NUYE

### Read and discover

- Browse published articles and search by keyword or tag.
- Check an article's summary, then learn from its code, diagrams, tables, and quizzes.
- Find articles you've read in your reading history and return to them later.
- Start with a ready-made outline: **Technical explainer**, **Code tutorial**, or **Visual summary**.

### Write and explain

- Use the visual editor to combine headings, text, quotes, lists, tables, code, and images—no Markdown knowledge required.
- Arrange text, speech bubbles, shapes, drawings, images, code, and quizzes on a free-position canvas. Move and resize items to build your layout. Canvas text also supports Markdown.
- Add a title, summary, category, tags, and cover image. Preview the reader's view before publishing.
- Your edits are saved automatically. Publishing creates an article URL you can share.

### Make more of what you read

- Try commenting, liking, following an author, saving an article, and sharing its link from an article page.
- Save glossary terms to your personal page.
- Article quizzes support multiple-choice and short-answer questions.

## Getting started

1. Choose **Read articles** to browse, or **Write an article** to create.
2. Sign up or log in to view your articles, reading history, and saved glossary terms on your personal page.
3. Choose a template or create an article in the editor. Preview it before publishing.

On a phone, use your browser's **Share** menu (iPhone/iPad) or browser menu (Android) and select **Add to Home Screen** to add NUYE's icon to your home screen.

## Saving and demo features

Without a Supabase connection, NUYE runs in demo mode and saves accounts, articles, reading history, and related data in that browser. This data is not shared with another browser or device. To keep demo data, continue using the same browser.

With Supabase configured, account authentication and article/image storage are available. Comments are still stored in the browser, and likes, follows, article saves, and quiz responses are currently demo interactions. They are not shared with other readers or guaranteed to persist between visits. Article summaries and glossary entries are sample content, not generated by an external AI service.

## For operators and developers

Install Node.js, then start the app locally:

```sh
npm install
npm run dev
```

To connect Supabase, copy the settings shown in `.env.example` into `.env.local`:

```env
VITE_SUPABASE_URL=https://YOUR_PROJECT.supabase.co
VITE_SUPABASE_ANON_KEY=YOUR_SUPABASE_ANON_KEY
```

Run `supabase/migrations/202611050001_initial_schema.sql` in the Supabase Dashboard SQL Editor. Only configure the Anon Key in the frontend; never put the Service Role Key there.
